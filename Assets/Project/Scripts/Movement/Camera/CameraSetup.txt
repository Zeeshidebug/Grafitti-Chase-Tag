MOVEMENT CAMERA

Configured scenes: Gameplay, AI-NAV-TEST2, AI-Nav-Test.

Input ownership
- CinemachineInputAxisController drives only OrbitalFollow X/Y.
- The orbit uses WorldSpace and wraps horizontally. Radius, target height,
  recentering and damping use each scene's Inspector settings.
- RotationComposer aims at that height without a second Look input.
- PlayerMovementIntent READS the rendered camera basis for WASD movement.
  It never writes camera rotation or reads Look. Its delayed facing affects
  the actor only. Input runs before intent, then movement.

MovementCameraFeedback (on CinemachineCamera)
- Sprint: up to +5 vertical FOV degrees, 0.20 s enter / 0.35 s recover.
  Requires sprint intent, available stamina and actual movement. Grounded
  sprint has a short 0.15 s airborne grace. A blocked player gets no boost.
- Slide: up to +7 degrees TOTAL, camera/focus lowered 0.45 m. Timing is
  0.10 s enter / 0.22 s recover. Remains lowered until SlideExecutor finishes,
  including when a low ceiling prevents standing.
- Landing: up to 0.08 m dip, recovers in 0.18 s; ignores small steps.
- Vault: hold the entry camera position, lift 0.30 m, widen FOV by 8 degrees.
  Enter in 0.12 s, return to the live orbit in 0.65 s, recover FOV in 0.45 s.
- Climb: lift 0.45 m and widen FOV by 7 degrees. Enter in 0.15 s, return
  in 0.80 s, recover FOV in 0.55 s. The shot lasts until the executor finishes,
  including extra time spent waiting at a blocked ledge.
- During either shot the composer aims at the moving player. Mouse input
  updates the eventual orbit. Recovery tracks that orbit even if the player
  runs away; another traversal starts from the current visible position.
- TicTac and Wall Rebound share Launch Shot. A successful launch holds the
  entry position for 0.15 s, then arcs behind the outgoing movement direction
  over 0.50 s with a 4 m lift. FOV widens by 5 degrees from launch. The
  camera stays behind the player for 0.20 s before returning over 1.50 s.
  FOV recovers in 1 s. A new launch restarts the shot from the visible pose.
- Pole Shot orbits the executor's pole centre and aims at the composer's
  usual height above it. The default lift is 5 m with +5 degrees FOV over
  0.50 s. Pole Turn Fraction follows 25% of the player's actual angular
  travel, capped by Pole Max Degrees Per Second (60). Releasing the pole or
  exhausting spin stamina starts the 1.50 s return and 1 s FOV recovery.
- The shared launch and pole shots return along an arc to the live mouse orbit.
- Vault Shot and Climb Shot in the Inspector control those defaults per rig.
  Traversal Position Intensity scales both hold and lift. At 0.5 the camera
  follows half the orbit displacement; at zero it follows normally.
  Dynamic Fov Intensity controls all FOV effects. Set all four intensities
  to zero to disable movement feedback.
- Defaults are starting values proposed for this project, not copied game
  constants. Vertical FOV is additive to the authored lens (currently 60).
- TraversalOrbitalFollow is the rig's position component. It runs the usual
  Cinemachine orbit and applies the shot before Deoccluder and RotationComposer.
  Deoccluder can pull a held camera forward to keep sight of the actor.
- Follow must resolve to a PlayerMovementIntent. Switching to an AI target
  applies no player feedback. Disable/rebind/warp clears transient state.
  On respawn notify Cinemachine with OnTargetObjectWarped; ResetFeedback is
  also available for explicit cuts. Large discontinuities reset sampling.

Collision
- TraversalDeoccluder runs Cinemachine's obstruction checks from the framed
  focus. A lone active pole is transparent along that sightline. Walls are
  checked across the full distance. Other poles on the sightline and camera
  contact with the pole use the rig's normal collision policy.
- Included layers: Default, Parkour, walkableSurface, poleLayer,
  wallRebound, buildings. Entity is excluded; trigger tokens are ignored
  by Cinemachine physics queries. Set environment colliders to these layers.
- Pull forward immediately when obstructed, recover in 0.20 s, radius 0.4.
- Keep focus/target offsets purely vertical so actor yaw cannot rotate them.

Verification
- Editor Test Runner: MovementCameraTests in Scripts/0_Dummy/Editor
  (EditMode suite enters Play Mode).
- The integration fixture clones the actual Gameplay player and camera into
  an isolated physics arena and exercises real keyboard/mouse input.
- Save work before running. The suite refuses unsaved scenes and restores
  the saved scene setup after each check.
- Manually tune framing in the full arena with sprint, slide under bars,
  jumping, vault/climb, wall rebound, pole spin, tokens and camera corners.
  The fixture verifies behavior; it does not establish final gameplay feel.

Design references
- Apex: restrained sprint feedback; EA recommends minimal sprint view shake.
  https://help.ea.com/en/articles/apex-legends/best-settings-pc/
- Rainbow Six: independent visual intensity/comfort controls.
  https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/r2MFilRathMnz0nZ3zosN/rainbow-six-siege-accessibility-spotlight
- Deadlock is a third-person movement/framing reference only; no claim that
  these parameters reproduce its internal camera implementation.
- Cinemachine APIs were checked against installed 3.1.5 package source.
